Chart encodings

Every chart lines up one column per day, so readers can scan down a date. Blue shows measurements, green and magenta show status, and shape shows data quality.

Icons

Use Lucide icons at stroke width 2.75, in the current text colour, at 16px inline or 18–22px standalone. Use icons only where they help scanning (brand mark, ask, consultation, export). Don't add decorative icons to data.

Voice & copy

Factual, dated and honest about limits. Clinicians read it in a minute; families read it with them.
